Multiverse
    
Ingredients
Serving: 1

1 ounce Kevin Kos’ melon liqueur
3/4 ounce white rum, preferably Hamilton White ’Stache
3/4 ounce aged aquavit, preferably Linie
3/8 ounce acid-adjusted orange juice (see Editor’s Note)
3/8 ounce acid-adjusted pineapple juice (see Editor’s Note)
1/4 ounce pistachio orgeat, preferably Orgeat Works
5 drops saline solution (4:1, water to salt)
Garnish: disco ball keychain (optional)

Directions
Combine all ingredients in a shaker.
Add ice (preferably one large shaking cube and two Kold-Draft cubes), seal, and shake until well-chilled.
Strain into a chilled Nick & Nora glass.
Garnish with a disco ball keychain.
Editor's Note
Acid-Adjusted Pineapple Juice
100 milliliters pineapple juice, strained
3.2 grams citric acid
2 grams malic acid

In a container, stir all ingredients to combine.

Acid-Adjusted Orange Juice
100 milliliters orange juice, strained
3.2 grams citric acid
2 grams malic acid

In a container, stir all ingredients to combine.

Ingredients
Serving: 1

1 1/2 ounces Calvados, preferably Lemorton Reserve
1/2 ounce yellow Chartreuse
1/2 ounce lime juice
1/2 ounce pineapple gum syrup (see Editor’s Note)

Directions
Combine all ingredients in a shaker.
Add ice and shake until chilled.
Double-strain into a chilled coupe.
Editor's Note
Pineapple Gum Syrup
340 grams sugar
17 grams gum arabic
400 milliliters pineapple juice

Sift the gum arabic into the sugar. Heat the pineapple juice to 180°F, and slowly add the sugar mixture, stirring continuously until fully dissolved. Strain the mixture through cheesecloth and let it sit overnight in the refrigerator. Skim off the white film that forms on the top, then bottle. Keeps, refrigerated, for about 1 month.

Ingredients
Serving: 1

1 ounce strawberry-infused Campari (see Editor's Note)
1 ounce bianco vermouth
1 ounce gin, preferably Tanqueray
Garnish: half strawberry

Directions
Combine all ingredients in a mixing glass over ice and stir until chilled.

Editor's Note
Strawberry-Infused Campari:
1 quart sliced strawberries
750mL Campari

Combine ingredients in a nonreactive container and allow them to infuse for three to four days. Strain out the solids and rebottle.
