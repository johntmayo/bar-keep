NAME: Pineapple Mojito No. 2

INGREDIENTS:
- 14 fresh Mint leaves
- 50 ml Light white rum
- 22.5 ml Pineapple juice
- 15 ml Pineapple liqueur
- 15 ml Lime juice
- 15 ml Soda (club soda) water

INSTRUCTIONS:
Select and pre-chill a COLLINS GLASS.
Prepare garnish of pineapple wedge and mint sprigs bouquet.
ADD first 4 ingredients to chilled glass and two-thirds fill with crushed ice.
CHURN and the fill glass with more crushed ice.
TOP with soda.
Garnish with mint sprigs bouquet and pineapple wedge on rim.
Serve with a straw.



NAME: Slynx

INGREDIENTS:
- 2.5 ml Mezcal
- 45 ml Reposado tequila
- 15 ml Applejack brandy
- 15 ml Pear liqueur
- 1 dash Barrel-aged bitters

INSTRUCTIONS:
Select and pre-chill an OLD-FASHIONED GLASS.
Prepare garnish of pear wedge.
RINSE chilled glass with mezcal (swirl around to coat and discard excess) and set to one side.
STIR other ingredients with ice.
STRAIN into mezcal-rinsed glass.
FILL glass with ice.
Garnish with pear wedge.

Yellow Bird
    
Ingredients
Serving: 1

1 1/2 ounces white rum, such as Street Pumas Panamanian Rum or Plantation 3 Star Rum
3/4 ounce Galliano L’Autentico
1/2 ounce Cointreau
1/2 ounce lime juice
1 teaspoon acid lime cordial (see Editor's Note)
5 drops saline (see Editor's Note)
Garnish: lime twist

Directions
Combine all ingredients in a mixing tin and shake with one large ice cube and two small ice cubes.
Strain into a chilled coupe.
Garnish with lime twist.
Editor's Note
Acid Lime Cordial:
Combine 1000 grams clarified lime juice, 1000 grams white sugar and the peels of three limes in a saucepan. Simmer until the lime oil has been extracted and the sugar is dissolved. Blend with 14.5 grams of citrus acid and 7.5 grams of malic acid per finished liter of syrup. Demerara syrup can be substituted for cordial, if necessary.

Saline:
Combine 80 gams of water and 20 grams of salt and stir to combine.
